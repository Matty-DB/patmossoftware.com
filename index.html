AI-created training plans

Use the AI you choose. Keep training in RunFlow.

RunFlow helps you create a personalized running plan with an AI chat app you already use, then brings that plan into your training calendar.

AI plan writing happens in Claude, ChatGPT, or another AI you choose, not inside RunFlow.

  1. Copy RunFlow’s prompt

    It asks about your goal, recent running, available days, and other details needed for a plan.

  2. Create the plan with your AI

    Use the prompt with Claude, ChatGPT, or another chat AI. Bring back its structured training plan.

  3. Import, review, and start

    Paste the reply or choose a JSON/text file. Check the plan, then save it to RunFlow with its sessions and schedule.

RunFlow questions

Before your first run.

Is RunFlow available to download yet?

Not yet. RunFlow is coming soon for iPhone; there is no release date to announce yet.

Does RunFlow create AI training plans inside the app?

No. RunFlow gives you a prompt to use with an AI chat service you choose. You bring the resulting plan back to RunFlow by pasting it or importing a file.

Does RunFlow track running routes with GPS?

No. The first release does not use GPS or record routes. You enter your run distance yourself.
