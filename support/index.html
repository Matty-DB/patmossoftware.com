Shikaku

How do I play?

Divide the grid into rectangles. Every rectangle holds exactly one number, and that number is how many squares it covers. When the whole grid is covered, the puzzle is solved.

Drag from one corner to the other to draw a rectangle. Tap a rectangle you have already drawn to remove it. If you change your mind mid‑drag, come back to the square you started on and let go: that cancels, and costs nothing.

Why was my rectangle rejected when it looked right?

A rectangle can obey every rule and still make the rest of the puzzle impossible, leaving a number with nowhere to fit, or a square that nothing can reach. Shikaku catches that at the move that causes it, rather than letting you find out twenty moves later.

What do the marks in the corner mean?

Mistakes remaining. You get three per puzzle. Mis‑swipes and cancelled drags never count. Only genuine reasoning errors do.

I bought the full game and it has not unlocked

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore purchases. That re-checks your purchase with Apple and unlocks it on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. If it still does not appear, email us.

I lost my progress

Progress is stored on your device, so deleting the app deletes it. Your purchase is not lost: reinstall and use Restore purchases.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple, so refunds go through them at reportaproblem.apple.com. We are not able to issue refunds ourselves.

Does the Daily Challenge cost anything?

No. It is free, and stays free, whether or not you buy the full game.

Is my data collected?

No. Nothing is collected and nothing is sent anywhere. See the privacy policy.
